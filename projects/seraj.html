Seraj

Overview

Seraj is an augmented reality cultural exploration game designed to help users discover Saudi heritage through interactive, location-based experiences. The project blends storytelling with technology, allowing visitors to point their devices at heritage sites and instantly access historical context, visuals, and guided narration.

Key Features

  • Augmented reality overlays that bring historical sites to life.
  • Interactive maps highlighting nearby cultural landmarks.
  • Bilingual content (Arabic and English) for a wider audience.
  • Simple, accessible interface designed for visitors of all ages.

Tools

  • Figma for UI/UX prototyping.

My Contribution

As part of the team, I contributed to the user interface prototype and visual design, focusing on making the experience intuitive for first-time users. I also contributed to shaping the platform’s visual identity, aiming for a modern design that remained respectful of the cultural content it presents. The prototype was created using Figma.
